Context, not a cartography dump

The map stays complete.
The prompt stays focused.

IW Spatial keeps geography, locations, paths, and areas in hidden script-readable trackers. A refresh step calculates exactly what matters around the player's current coordinates and exposes one compact local context to the Storyteller.

Built for spatial storytelling

Mechanics precise enough
to disappear into the story.

The toolkit handles the geometry and relationships. Your world decides when movement becomes authoritative and what that movement means.

Coordinate-first navigation

Absolute x_km, y_km, and z_m coordinates remain authoritative. Named anchors and active paths add detail without replacing position.

Free roaming ready

Typed paths

Model roads, trails, rivers, creeks, and streams with ordered navigation points, elevation, direction, and exact proximity.

Polygonal areas

Resolve every containing polygon or multipolygon, including nested semantics and true geometric holes.

Sparse spatial index

Candidate grids reduce runtime search work while exact distances, projections, and polygon membership remain precise.

Compact local context

Return validated location detail, current areas, and the nearest discovered locations and paths—bounded by configurable limits.

Validation built in

Catch broken references, invalid coordinates, path-order problems, malformed polygons, cycles, and inconsistent initial anchors.
